# Ida Rolf — attribution and completeness notes

Research snapshot: 2026-09-17

## Psycho-Logics and Posture (1958)

This source should **not yet be counted uncritically as a book authored by Ida Rolf**.

Later Dr. Ida Rolf Institute material gives the citation:

> Wood, Denis Lawson and Ida P. Rolf. 1958. *Psycho-logics and Posture: Postural Dynamics of Dr. Ida P. Rolf, and Psychology of Personal Constructs of Dr. George A. Kelly considered as related and complementary non-elementalistic methods.* United Kingdom: Ashingdon.

Other bibliographies and surviving bookseller/catalog records list only Denis Lawson-Wood as the author. A 2004 Rolf Institute reprint is also indexed under Lawson-Wood.

Working interpretation: the monograph is unquestionably important as an early published description of Rolf's Postural Dynamics / emerging ten-session method, but the exact authorship relationship needs verification from the 1958 title page and colophon before this repository labels it a Rolf-authored book.

Status: 1958 publication, therefore **not public domain merely by publication date in the United States**. No open license located. Until rights and authorship are resolved, keep metadata and an original summary only.

Summary: The monograph places Rolf's postural-dynamics method beside George Kelly's psychology of personal constructs as two related, non-elementalistic approaches. Later historical work treats it as one of the earliest published descriptions of the recognizable modern ten-session sequence. Its importance is therefore historical even if Lawson-Wood turns out to be the sole textual author: it can help reconstruct what Rolf was teaching by the late 1950s.

## Postural Dynamics teaching notes

Later historical work at the Dr. Ida Rolf Institute refers to student notes from Rolf classes in 1954, 1955, 1956, and 1957. These are primary records of her teaching but are **student notes, not writings by Rolf**, so they belong in a separate teaching-record corpus rather than the bibliography of authored works.

The 1957 notes are described by later archival researchers as already resembling the mature ten-session 'Recipe'; earlier notes show changes especially in sessions five, six, and seven. These may be more informative for developmental history than later recollections, but provenance and transcription history must be retained.

## Bulletin completeness problem

A literature survey reports approximately 150 contributions to the *Bulletin for Structural Integration* between 1969 and 1980, with **21 contributions from Rolf**. The current online author index exposes fewer than 21 obvious records.

Do not convert '21 contributions' into '21 distinct authored articles' without issue-by-issue checking. The count can include:

- coauthored drafts;
- transcripts of discussions;
- speeches or remarks;
- introductions or editor-mediated texts;
- reprints;
- letters or short notices.

Next archival operation: enumerate every Bulletin issue, record title / issue / pages / byline / text type, and reconcile the 21-count against the online author index.
